THE PROBLEM
✗Cryptocurrency prices exhibit extreme volatility. Bitcoin dropped from $68K to $15.8K, down 74% in one year.
✗Traditional trading models are often unreliable under high-frequency non-linear price behavior.
✗Patterns are non-stationary and shift rapidly across market regimes.
✗External factors like sentiment and macro shocks strongly influence market direction.
RESEARCH QUESTIONS
Q1:What ML/DL models best predict short-term crypto prices using candle and volume data?
Q2:Can sequence models capture temporal patterns better than classical statistical methods?
Q3:Can predictions improve risk-adjusted metrics like Sharpe ratio?
Q4:How can this be deployed into a live dashboard with practical backtesting support?

METHODOLOGY
1. Data Collection & Preprocessing
Collected 4 years of hourly data and cleaned outliers/missing values.
2. Feature Engineering
Built 26 indicators including RSI, MACD, trend and volatility features.
3. Multi-Model Training
Trained LSTM/GRU and compared with ensemble and baseline ML methods.
4. Walk-Forward Backtest
Time-aware 80/20 split with rolling validation against baseline strategy.
5. Statistical Evaluation
Used MAE/RMSE/R2 plus directional accuracy for signal quality.
6. Deployment
Integrated inference workflow into dashboard for live usage.
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
DATA LAYERAPIs (CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance) to SQLite and CSV cache.
FEATURE ENGINEERINGRSI, MACD, Bollinger, lag features, normalization.
MODEL LAYERLSTM and GRU (TensorFlow), Random Forest, Linear Regression.
EVALUATIONMAE, RMSE, R2, Directional Accuracy and visual diagnostics.
DEPLOYMENTLive dashboard with API-backed prediction services.
MONITORINGDrift checks, performance tracking, periodic retraining loop.

CONCLUSION & FUTURE WORK
Finding: Across four coins, the strongest performance came from Linear Regression and ensemble-based methods rather than the heaviest architectures.
Contribution: The study delivers an end-to-end pipeline from data collection and feature engineering to backtesting and live API deployment.
Conclusion: Robust forecasting in crypto can be both accurate and deployable when model choice is guided by comparative evidence rather than model complexity alone.
Next step: expand feature enrichment with macroeconomic indicators (rates, CPI, DXY, and equity indices).
Next step: test Transformer/attention-based models and strengthen real-time risk controls.
